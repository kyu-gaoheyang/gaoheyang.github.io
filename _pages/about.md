I am a Ph.D. student in the Computer Vision Group at Kyushu University, advised by Prof. Hiroshi Kawasaki. My research focuses on underwater 3D reconstruction, laser-based structured light sensing, and visual SLAM for robotic perception in challenging aquatic environments.

My current research aims to develop accurate and robust underwater perception systems by combining active laser sensing, refractive geometry, deep learning, and multi-view geometric optimization. I am particularly interested in underwater laser-visual SLAM, multi-line structured-light reconstruction, laser stripe detection, and sensor calibration for robotic 3D perception.

My doctoral research is supported by the JST BOOST Fellowship, Next-Generation AI Human Resource Development Program, from April 2024 to March 2027.

## Research Interests

* Underwater 3D Reconstruction
* Laser-Visual SLAM
* Structured Light Sensing
* Refractive Geometry
* Deep Learning for Robot Perception
* Multi-Sensor Fusion
* Underwater Robot Navigation
