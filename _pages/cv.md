## Research Experience

### Underwater SLAM and 3D Reconstruction

**Researcher**, Kyushu University
2024 – Present

My current research focuses on underwater 3D reconstruction and robotic perception using camera-laser sensing systems. I work on active 3D imaging systems that combine RGB cameras, multiple line lasers, geometric calibration, laser stripe detection, and SLAM-based multi-view optimization.

Main topics include:

* Designing underwater active 3D sensing systems using cameras and multiple line lasers.
* Developing laser stripe detection and identification methods for challenging underwater images.
* Modeling geometric constraints from structured light and integrating them into SLAM and reconstruction pipelines.
* Studying calibration, error modeling, and multi-stage optimization for underwater laser-visual systems.
* Evaluating reconstruction accuracy and robustness in real underwater environments.

### Indoor Localization Using CNN and Reflected Visible Light

**Developer**, Hokkaido University
2023 – 2024

I developed a CNN-based indoor localization method using reflected visible light patterns captured by a rolling-shutter camera. The project involved simulation-based data generation, image-based position classification, and robustness evaluation under noise.

Main topics include:

* LED frequency encoding and reflected-light image simulation.
* CNN-based position recognition from rolling-shutter stripe patterns.
* Robustness analysis under Gaussian noise.
* Geometric analysis of light-source position estimation.

### Visual SLAM in Low-Texture Environments

**Developer**, Hokkaido University
2021 – 2022

I studied visual SLAM optimization in low-texture indoor environments, where point features are often insufficient for stable tracking. The work explored line features and Manhattan-world assumptions as additional geometric constraints.

Main topics include:

* Analysis of ORB-SLAM2 failure cases in low-texture environments.
* Line-feature extraction and geometric constraint modeling.
* Pose estimation improvement using structural regularities in indoor scenes.

### Speaker Verification Using LSTM and GE2E Loss

**Research Developer**
2019 – 2020

I implemented a speaker verification system based on MFCC features, LSTM networks, d-vector embeddings, cosine similarity, and Generalized End-to-End Loss.

### Underwater Image Enhancement

**Developer**
2019 – 2020

I studied traditional underwater image enhancement methods, including white balance, histogram equalization, and dark channel prior, and analyzed their limitations under color attenuation, scattering, and low-contrast underwater conditions.
